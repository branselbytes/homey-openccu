Bring Homematic IP into your everyday Homey Flows: automate lighting, heating and shutters, react to sensors, and keep an eye on your OpenCCU. OpenCCU Local is an independently developed fork of homey-matic, adapted for a direct local connection between OpenCCU and Homey Pro. It supports compatible Homematic IP wireless devices, selected Wired devices and heating groups. Use OpenCCU programs and system variables in Flows, and view system status and service messages in dashboard widgets. No MQTT, CCU-Jack, RedMatic or Home Assistant is required.

You need a Homey Pro running Homey software 12.9.0 or newer and an OpenCCU reachable on your local network. Configure the connection in the app settings before adding devices. If no matching device or device family is listed, try "Generic OpenCCU device". Available features depend on the datapoints the app can detect and map; full functionality is not guaranteed. The app is in beta, and some models still need testing with real hardware. Devices from the previous Homematic Homey app must be paired again in this app.
